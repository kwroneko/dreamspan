# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Start a story with one character or a group, stream replies, and keep alternate replies, edits, branches and side threads.
- Bring over a SillyTavern data folder or import individual character cards, lorebooks and chat-completion presets. Export a chat or take your library with you in SillyTavern format.
- Keep long stories within the model's context budget with automatic summaries. Open a summary to see which messages it covers, how it was written and what the request cost when pricing is available.
- Connect your own hosted or local models. Choose defaults for new chats or pick a model and prompt preset for a particular story.
- Try workshops to work on cards, lore, prompts, stories and files with an assistant. MCP connections and plugins are available for advanced setups.
